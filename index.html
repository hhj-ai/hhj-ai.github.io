Research

I'm broadly interested in how vision should be learned, represented, and scaled. I tend to see most problems through representation learning, shaped by three things: the objective, the data, and the architecture. Questions I'm currently working on include: what visual information survives different training paradigms (contrastive pretraining, VLM fine-tuning, native multimodal training); whether pixel-level and semantic objectives can coexist in one representation; and how to give the visual pathway real supervision in VLM post-training, so models answer from what they see rather than from language priors.
