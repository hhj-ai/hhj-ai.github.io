Research

I'm interested in representation learning, computer vision, multimodal AI, and machine learning. Currently, I focus on making vision a first-class citizen in vision-language models — models that answer from what they actually see, rather than from language priors.

Evidence-RL: Towards Evidence-intensive Visual Reasoning
Haojie Huang*, Xinlei Yu*, Chengming Xu*, Zhangquan Chen, Cheng Yang, Qingdong He, Yu Yang, Jiangning Zhang, Xiaobin Hu†
NeurIPS, 2026
project page / arXiv

Counterfactual Evidence Disentanglement (CED) rewards VLMs during RL post-training only when their answers causally depend on the right visual evidence, improving grounded reasoning across nine benchmarks with no inference-time cost.
